# Contributor Covenant Code of Conduct

## Our pledge

We pledge to make participation in this project a harassment-free experience
for everyone, regardless of age, disability, ethnicity, gender identity,
experience level, nationality, appearance, race, religion, or sexual identity.

## Expected behavior

Be respectful, constructive, and precise. Welcome different viewpoints, accept
good-faith corrections, and focus criticism on code and behavior rather than
people.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, doxxing, sexualized conduct, and sharing
private credentials or personal data are not acceptable.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may remove comments, reject contributions, or restrict
participation when conduct violates this policy. Report concerns privately
through the security/contact channel in the repository.
